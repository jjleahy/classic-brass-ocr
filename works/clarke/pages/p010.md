## THIRD STUDY

Practice without repeating at first, until the fingers are under perfect control.

These Exercises are excellent for training the lips to be flexible in slurring, Single and Double Tonguing, especially towards the end of the Study.

ETUDE III can be played entirely in one breath with practice.
