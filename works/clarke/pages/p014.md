## FOURTH STUDY

These Exercises were written to help overcome obstacles whereby the Whole Tone Trill is so often abused and played clumsily on the Cornet.

By careful practice the intervals will sound clearly in the different registers, and you can overcome imperfections so common in the construction of many cornets; such as the Interval from low B♮ to C♯, in Ex. No. 71; also C to D in Ex. No. 72.

The fingers as well as the lips must be elastic.

Single and Double Tongue them after you have made sufficient progress in slurring them perfectly.

Try to play ETUDE IV in one breath, it is possible.

Met. ♩ = 100 to 144
