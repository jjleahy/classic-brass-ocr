# Clarke: transcription notes

Remarks on points where the transcription follows the printed page and the reader might
expect otherwise, or where it simplifies the printed layout. They are reviewed and confirmed
against the scans. Pages are cited by label (`p009` is printed page 9).

## p009, no. 37, bar 4: no accents

Bars 1–3 carry accents on the first note of each group of four, but bar 4 has none. This is
as printed, and it is kept unaccented. (The accents return in no. 38.)

## Fermatas over the final double bar are omitted

In most of the book, the fermata at the end of an exercise stands over the final double bar
rather than over a note. These fermatas are not transcribed. The few fermatas printed over a
note are the exceptions and are transcribed.
