# Arban: Complete Celebrated Method for the Cornet

*Arban's Complete Celebrated Method for the Cornet*, "New Revised and Authentic Edition",
newly revised and edited by Edwin Franko Goldman. Published by Carl Fischer, New York.
The text is printed in parallel English, German and French. Sources are listed in the
[README](../../README.md#arban-grande-méthode-complète-de-cornet-à-pistons).

## Page labels

| Labels | Pages |
|---|---|
| `f01`–`f02` | Cover; list of the principal words used in modern music |
| `r01`–`r06` | Front matter, printed I–VI: report, biographical sketch, preface, illustrations |
| `p001`–`p347` | Printed pages 1–347. The numbering runs continuously across the five scan files. |
