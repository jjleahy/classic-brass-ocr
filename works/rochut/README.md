# Rochut: 120 Melodious Etudes for Trombone

*120 Melodious Etudes for Trombone*, from the Vocalises of Marco Bordogni, selected and
transcribed by Joannès Rochut. Published by Carl Fischer, New York, 1928, in three books.
Sources are listed in the
[README](../../README.md#rochut-120-melodious-etudes-for-trombone).

## Page labels

Page numbers restart in each book, so every label carries its book:

| Labels | Book | Etudes |
|---|---|---|
| `b1-p002`–`b1-p087` | Book One | Nos. 1–60 |
| `b2-p001`–`b2-p065` | Book Two | Nos. 61–90; `b2-p001` is the Foreword |
| `b3-p002`–`b3-p079` | Book Three | Nos. 91–120 |

Numbers in `[brackets]` in `catalog/pages.csv` are not printed on the page; they were
inferred from the sequence.

A table mapping each etude number to its music ID will be added once the transcription is
complete.
